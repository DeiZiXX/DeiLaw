DeiLaw - Privacy and network communication
==========================================

This program is unofficial, is not affiliated with RMRP, and can contact
third-party online services depending on your choices.

Automatic background connections are OFF by default on a new installation.
You can optionally enable the following on the next page:
- GitHub: automatic checking of DeiLaw updates and release history.
- RMRP websites: automatic refreshing of in-game legislation.
- Supabase: polling for replies to your support reports.

Requests explicitly initiated by you may contact:
- Supabase for Discord or guest authentication, reviews, reports and Cloud AI.
- Discord for login; OpenAI via the Supabase backend for Cloud AI queries.
- RMRP websites for law searches and updates.
- GitHub for manual update checks and viewing release history.

The data shared can include your submitted text, limited conversation context,
account/session identifiers and user-supplied attachments. Read PRIVACY.md
in the source repository for the full details and available controls:
https://github.com/DeiZiXX/DeiLaw/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

By continuing you acknowledge seeing this notice. All optional background
checks may remain OFF. You can change them later in DeiLaw settings.
